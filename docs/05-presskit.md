## Description (short)
HUE & CLAIM mixes the classic "draw a line, claim the land" arcade formula with modern roguelite buildcrafting. Leave your land, draw a loop through a world drained of color and come back home: everything inside blooms into color and every monster inside is captured. But enemies that touch your line light a spark that races toward you — make it home first.

## Description (long)
The Grey has drained Chromara of every color. As Pip, the last painter, you reclaim the world one stroke at a time. Each stage is a procedurally generated canvas; paint enough of it and the biome's boss appears — a monster you can only truly hurt by drawing tight loops around it.
Every run you assemble a build from weapons that evolve, passives that stack and relics that bend the rules. Between runs you spend the pigments you gathered in the Atelier — mixing Crimson, Azure and Ochre into rarer secondary colors to unlock new techniques, relics and painters.

## Key features
- A mechanic anyone understands in three seconds: draw, loop, claim.
- Risk in every stroke: sparks race along your trail toward you.
- Capture entire hordes at once — grazing herds notice you only after a moment.
- Five biomes that change the rules (bogs, walled ruins, lava fissures, a fading void).
- Bosses you have to out-draw.
- Color-theory meta progression: mix pigments to unlock new options.
- 100 % procedural art and synthesized audio — tiny download, runs everywhere.
