# HUE & CLAIM — Steam Store Page (English, primary)

## App name
HUE & CLAIM

## Short description (≤ 300 characters)
Draw the line. Claim the world. A territory-claiming roguelite: paint loops through a world drained of color, trap entire hordes in a single stroke, and outrun the sparks racing along your trail. Build wild synergies, topple bosses and repaint Chromara.

[h2]One stroke. Eighty monsters. Pure color.[/h2]
The world of Chromara has been drained by the Grey. You are Pip, the last painter. Step off your land, draw a line through the Grey and come back home — everything inside your loop bursts into color, and every monster trapped inside gets [b]captured[/b].

But the Grey fights back. The moment an enemy touches your line, a [b]spark[/b] ignites and races along it toward you. Make it home first and the land is yours. Get caught and your stroke [b]snaps[/b].

[*][b]Bosses you have to out-draw:[/b] the tighter your loop around a boss, the harder it hits. The Smudge King, the Eraser, the Cinder Wyrm and more each break the rules differently.
